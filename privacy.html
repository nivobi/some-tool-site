SOME-TOOL

Privacy Policy

Effective October 2, 2026

SOME-TOOL is a desktop application that runs on your own computer. This policy explains what information it handles and where that information goes.

No servers, no tracking

SOME-TOOL has no backend server. We do not collect, receive, store, sell or share your personal information, and the app contains no analytics or advertising.

Information the app handles on your computer

  • Your videos and captions. Stored in a folder you choose on your computer.
  • TikTok account information. When you sign in with TikTok, the app receives your basic profile (open ID, display name and avatar) so it can show which account a video will be uploaded to. It is stored only on your computer.
  • Access tokens. The sign-in tokens TikTok issues are stored in your operating system's secure credential store (for example Windows Credential Manager or macOS Keychain), not in plain files.

Where information is sent

  • TikTok. When you choose to upload a video, the video file and its caption are sent to TikTok so it appears as a draft in your TikTok account. TikTok's handling of that content is covered by TikTok's Privacy Policy.
  • Your AI assistant, if you use one. If you use Claude Code to help write captions, still frames from your video and your caption text are processed by Anthropic under your own Claude account and Anthropic's terms. This is optional.

Nothing is sent anywhere else.

Deleting your data

  • Delete the SOME-TOOL folder on your computer to remove your videos, captions and account list.
  • Remove the stored tokens from your operating system's credential store, or disconnect the account in the app.
  • Revoke SOME-TOOL's access at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.

Children

SOME-TOOL is not intended for anyone under 18.
