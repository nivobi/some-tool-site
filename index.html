SOME-TOOL

Caption and upload your own videos, from your desktop.

SOME-TOOL is a desktop tool for video creators. It runs on your own computer.

Privacy Policy Terms of Service

What it does

  • Keeps a folder of your finished videos and the captions you write for each of your accounts.
  • Helps you write captions that fit each account's style, which you review and edit before anything is uploaded.
  • Uploads a video you choose to your TikTok account as a draft. You then open TikTok, edit if you like, and post it yourself.

How TikTok is used

  • Login Kit: you sign in with TikTok so the tool can show which account a video will go to (display name and avatar).
  • Content Posting API, upload to drafts: after you approve a caption and click upload, the video is sent to your TikTok inbox as a draft.

The tool only acts on your own accounts, and only when you tell it to.
